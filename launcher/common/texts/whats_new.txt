0.1.4: more vibration. Still a test release: please keep sending your logs.

0.1.4 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game:
from 0.1.3 it builds again only what changed, from an older version a few minutes). Your saves and options are kept.

New
- Extra vibration (the VR menu's Controls, the launcher's VR Controls), three choices:
  - Off: the vibration as in 0.1.3.
  - Pickups (to start): also a buzz for each thing you collect: a light tick for an orb, an eco pill, ammo or a
    skull gem, a longer one for eco, a strong one for a power cell, a scout fly, a gun or a crystal.
  - The whole world: pickups, and each step (in the foot's hand), each jump, landing, roll and slide, each punch,
    kick, dive and flip, each hit that lands, and the world's shakes (explosions, quakes, bosses).
  Vibration strength sets how hard these are too, and Vibration off turns them off with the rest.
- Logs (Settings, Log): Save log writes everything for a bug report in one file: the app and the headset, each game's
  options, its last crash, the start and the end of its newest log and the run before, the preparation's messages and
  the app's own log. No saves or game files. Report a problem still makes its zip.

Changed
- The Quest's Graphics start as a player's tested Quest 3 has them: Refresh rate 120 Hz (or the most your headset
  has), Smoother frames on, Foveated rendering High, CPU and GPU boost off, Anti-aliasing and Anisotropic filtering off,
  Sharpening Normal. For a game with no options saved yet: options you set are kept. Balanced sets these. The PC's
  are as before.

Fixed
- Quest: the game's log no longer fills with the graphics chip's notes (thousands of lines by the sea), so the lines
  that matter are kept and less is written in play.

0.1.3: Jak II multiplayer, and fixes for the players' reports on 0.1.2. Still a test release: please keep sending
your logs.

0.1.3 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game,
a few minutes, no disc image needed). Your saves and options are kept. From 0.1.3 on, the PC builds again only what
an update changed, so the updates after this one are quicker.

New
- Jak II multiplayer: the community's Jak and Daxter II Multiplayer mod, built in. The title's Multiplayer hosts a
  game or joins one on your network or by address, with every VR feature for everyone and the others shown with their
  VR head and hands. With players of this port only. The launcher's Mods turns it off (then nothing of it runs), as
  Jak 1's multiplayer.
- A crosshair for the guns (Jak II and 3): a mark where the shot would land, on the wall, the ground or the enemy the
  gun points at, the same size near or far, red on an enemy the gun can reach. It starts as a small dot. The VR menu
  and the launcher's VR options have Crosshair (Off, Dot, Cross, Cross and dot, Circle, Circle and dot, Plus),
  Crosshair size, Crosshair color, Crosshair opacity and Crosshair red on enemies.
- Typing with the sticks in a text field (a name, an address): up or down changes the letter, right keeps it, left
  erases one, A enters it and Y leaves.
- Texture packs have their own tab (Texture packs, beside Mods), with Import: a pack you downloaded, as its .zip or
  the folder you unpacked it to (on PC, drop either onto the launcher too). It goes to the game it is for, on, to turn
  off, order or remove there. Packs made for OpenGOAL work; a pack for the PCSX2 emulator, or a .7z or .rar, is told
  apart with what to do. Find texture packs lists the ones in the mod lists.
- PC: the games can be kept on another drive. The Play tab's Where the games are kept moves them (up to about 10 GB a
  game): copied, checked, then removed from where they were. Saves and options stay where they are.

Fixed
- Jak 1's Load Game loads your save (it asked to write over it, and Yes started a new game there).
- Menus: the pointer and the sticks work in every menu of all three games, the trigger clicks with the pointer, and
  the stick's row stays chosen until you point at another. B goes back in the games' menus. Jak II's title: the stick
  and the pointer no longer land on the hidden Secrets row. Every kind of setting (On / Off, lists, sliders) takes the
  pointer, and a long settings page no longer scrolls on by itself when it opens with your hand resting low.
- Both eyes see the same: shiny walls, doors and lanterns, cloth, trails and lasers are drawn alike in each eye. Jak
  3's clothing no longer sticks to your view or the HUD.
- Posters and signs on walls hold steadier against the wall in VR (Jak II's city). The city's lit signs flicker by
  design, as in the game.
- Each shot, its flash and its light start at the gun in your hand.
- Punch and shoot (Jak II and 3's combo, the gun course's lesson): the shot goes to what the punch hit. On foot the
  left fist is only a fist now; Dark Jak (and Jak 3's Light Jak) is the left stick's click there.
- Dark Jak is as solid as Jak; the gear on his arms and legs fades with them near your eyes.
- The JET-Board: a quick squeeze of the right grip takes it out and puts it away, as does the right stick's click; a
  quick click is no longer lost, and it no longer pauses the game with your left thumb on its rest.

Riding
- First person in vehicles is on to start, with Vehicle view height, Vehicle view forward and Vehicle view tilt to put
  the view where you like it.
- Steady view when riding (on to start): the view stays in his seat without each bounce and shake (Jak 3's leaper
  lizards).
- Jak 1's Zoomer in first person: the view no longer drops into the Zoomer at each bump.
- Jak 3's gun turrets (Spargus' wall): you stand at the gun, at Jak's own height, looking over its barrels (the view
  sank into the turret as you played).

Adaptive cutscenes go back to Jak's eyes when a long scene comes back to him, without going back and forth.

Performance (each in the launcher's Graphics)
- Game frame rate: Automatic (to start): the headset's rate while the game keeps up, else 60.
- Dynamic resolution on to start. Share work between eyes (on): the second eye skips what is the same for both.
- Show performance (off): a small line low in your view with the frame rate and each frame's work.
- Less log writing in play.

0.1.1: fixes for the first players' reports, and a pass over how Jak's body feels around you.

Games prepared with 0.1.0 need preparing again (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game).
Your saves and options are kept.

Fixed
- Jak 1's menus: the stick moves through them again, so Load Game loads (it could start a new game by mistake).
- The menu pointer: a hand held still no longer takes back the row the stick chose, on every menu of all three games.
- A long settings page no longer scrolls on by itself when it opens with your hand resting low.
- Jak 1's list settings (language, frame rate, resolution and the like) take the pointer while you change them.
- Jak II no longer crashes as the sewer's gun turret is destroyed; Jak 3 no longer crashes in the arena or at the end
  of its tutorial.
- A scene that ends early lets Jak go again (Jak 1's pipe game in Lost Precursor City could leave him unable to move).
- The Quest no longer runs out of memory preparing Jak 3.
- Start works with Touch controllers on SteamVR: the left stick's click is the menu button there.
- Jak 3's title screen shows its text, and its opening no longer flickers.
- Jak 1's scenes seen through Jak's eyes face what happens.
- The HUD in Jak 1 and Jak 3, and Jak 3's ammo on the gun.
- Jak II's car: its height button works every time (the holsters count only on foot).
- Walking straight walks straight (it looked diagonal).
- Rolling, and punching for real in Jak II and 3, feel as they should.
- The options set at start reach the headset at once.

Your body
- Life size (World scale 100%, on to start): Jak as tall as you, his hands the size of yours.
- Keep your body upright (on): his body stands under your head through kicks, flips and landings.
- Fade body near your eyes (on): a part of him that comes too close fades smoothly instead of filling the view.
- Smoother arms, wrists, knees and fingers; fingers bend against walls and floors; Jak 1's arm wraps follow your hands.

New options (each in the VR menu and the launcher)
- Adaptive cutscenes (on): a scene goes around you by itself while Jak is not properly in it, and back to his eyes.
- Hold the left menu button one second in a scene to switch between his eyes and around you.
- First person in vehicles (off): the Zoomer, the JET-Board, the cars and the like from Jak's seat.
- Spin kick by spreading your arms (off).
- Buzz on d-pad presses and Buzz on gun switch (on).
- Holster size (130% to start: easier to put the gun away).
- Performance: Game frame rate (half rate), Dynamic resolution, PS2 detail, Sharpening and Highest foveation, all off
  or as before to start.

Graphics
- Smoother frames (AppSW) is off to start: it made the title screen flicker.
- Max draw distance now draws everything, shadows too.

Mods
- The Mods section says plainly what works in VR, with Browse mods: install, update, turn off and remove texture packs.
