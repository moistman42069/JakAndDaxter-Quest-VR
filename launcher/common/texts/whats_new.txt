0.1.6: Light Jak on the controllers, a smoother Quest, sharper Quest 3 graphics, and fixes for the latest reports.
Still a test release: please keep sending your logs.

0.1.6 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game,
which builds again only what changed). Your saves and options are kept.

New
- Light Jak (Jak 3), once the game has given him to you: hold the left grip and press Y (the game's "Hold L2"). Jak
  stops for his power; then let Y go to heal (the game's Triangle), slide your thumb from Y to X to slow time, press
  A for the wings or B for the shield, or push the right stick (up heals, down the wings, left slows time, right the
  shield). Let the grip go before choosing for Dark Jak, as on a gamepad. Dark Jak's quick squeeze of the left grip
  is as before, and as Dark Jak, Y is still his invisibility.
- Multiplayer on Jak 1's title screen and in its pause menu: host a game (from a save or a new game), join one from
  the games found on your network and the party server, see the players, leave. B goes back a page. Jak II's title
  has its Multiplayer (Host Game, Join Game) as before.

Changed
- The crosshair starts as the Dot again (0.1.5 started with none). A crosshair you picked yourself is kept.
- CPU and GPU boost is on to start, on the Quest too.
- Extra vibration starts at The whole world: every step, jump, landing, roll, hit and shake, and the pickups. If you
  had turned it Off, it stays off.
- Quest 3 and 3S start with sharper graphics: 150% eye resolution and Strong sharpening (with 120 Hz, Smoother
  frames, High foveation and Dynamic resolution as before). Quest 2 and Pro keep 100% eyes and Normal sharpening.
- Anti-aliasing and anisotropic filtering are off to start on every headset and the PC. A setting you changed
  yourself is kept.
- Smoother frames (Quest): the game draws half the headset's rate, as it should (at 120 Hz, 60 a second). It drew a
  quarter (30 a second), so every other frame had no new picture behind it.
- The game's own thread goes to the Quest as the app's main thread, which the Quest gives more of its time (it
  refused it as a worker).
- Dynamic resolution steadier: a step back up must hold for 10 seconds, not 3, so the eyes no longer swing between
  two sizes every few seconds.
- Jak 3's JET-Board: B grinds a rail (held) and X zaps, swapped from before, so your left thumb can stay on the stick
  while you grind. Jak II's board, and Jak 3 off the board, are as before.
- A button you are holding when the game gets the controllers back (the SteamVR dashboard or the Quest's menu
  closed, the headset put on again) counts only once you let it go, so it can't skip a scene or jump by itself.

Fixed
- A black flicker with Smoother frames on (Quest): the quarter-rate frames and the swinging eye size above.
- Leaving the game on the Quest (its menu, the headset taken off) could close it: the game tried to draw a flat
  screen at the Quest's full size. Now nothing is drawn while you are away and the game waits for you.
- Jak II: scenes stayed black for seconds at their start (since 0.1.5), and after a mission's load the screen could
  stay dark long after play had begun. The headset's black now ends with the game's.
- Jak 3: Torn's briefing in the Hip Hog could end in its first moments, black, and the mission went on without it.
  Triangle (Y) skips a Jak 3 scene only once it has run a second now, and the log says when a scene is skipped and
  when the game lost and got back the controllers.
- Jak 1: after the scene of any Boggy Swamp tether rock but the last, Jak stayed held where he stood and could not
  walk (since 0.1.5). The rocks' scene is the original game's again and lets him go.
- Jak 1: Jak grabbed no ledge of a level (since 0.1.0), so the climb to Klaww could not be made. One line of
  the multiplayer mod's ledge search was lost in our merge; it is back, and alone the search is the original
  game's.
- Jak 1 closed at Geyser Rock's scarecrows on a Quest 3: a type the scarecrow's break uses had been lost from the
  game's memory. If one is ever lost again the game puts it back and the log names it, and a crash report now names
  where it happened.
- Jak II's pause menu behind you (PC, SteamVR): the menu and the menu pointer are placed in the same space each frame,
  and the log says where the pause menu was put.
- A one-player game with the multiplayer mods on (Jak 1 and Jak II, as they start) is the original game again. Each
  change the mods make to the game's code was read against the original, and every one that reached a lone player
  now waits for a hosted or joined game. Among them, Jak II: the pause menu, Daxter's steps and the races' restarts,
  the tomb's boulder, enemies' hits and knock-backs, gems, orbs and crates, elevators and swing poles, the city's
  traffic. Jak 1: the swamp's tether rocks (above), the lift down to the sunken city, Load Game on an
  empty slot (it asked to write over it), the Zoomer's speed and Jak's collision after leaving a game, and the
  speedrun display's lines. Leaving a multiplayer game now puts everything of it away.
- Jak 1's opening stuttered on a Quest 3 in the short shot of Jak lying back on the boat, where the game draws all of
  Misty Island for the first time. Dynamic resolution (on to start) now draws the eyes smaller at once when most
  frames of a quarter second come late, instead of one step a second, so a sudden heavy shot settles within about a
  second.
- The VR menu: four rows' help ran into the hints under it (Extra vibration, Fists on the right grip, the multiplayer
  rows), and a semicolon showed as the Triangle button. Each fits in two lines now.
- Checked: Jak 1's breathing while you crouch is the game's own. His crouch animation plays its breathing sounds about
  once a second, the same with the crouch button as with your own crouch (and standing he breathes too), so it stays.

0.1.5: fixes for a tester's reports on 0.1.4, and a small circle crosshair. Still a test release: please keep sending
your logs.

0.1.5 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game,
which builds again only what changed). Your saves and options are kept.

New
- Crosshair: Small circle, a thin ring about half a degree across at Crosshair size 100%, for a precise aim.
- First person for each kind of ride: with First person in vehicles on, the VR menu's Body and the launchers' VR Body
  have a switch for each (Zoomer, Flut Flut, JET-Board, hover cars, hover bikes, Titan suit, leaper lizards, desert
  vehicles, other rides), each in the games that have it, all on to start.
- CONTROLS (the launchers' Controls page) shows what each button does in each game, on foot and in each ride.
- Every game button in reach: with the left hand held by your head, the left stick's click is Select (Jak II
  multiplayer's player list) and the right's L3 (its names and HUD), which on foot in Jak II and 3 had no button.
  Vive wands, which had no Select at all: the left touchpad's click in its middle, the hand by your head.

Changed
- Loads and blackouts (a scene starting or ending, a mission retried, the game's start) stay black in the headset
  however long they last, as on a flat screen: no more flat screen floating in front of you between scenes.
- B in the games' menus is the game's Circle where the menu has a Circle of its own: Jak 1's pause pages (B opens
  the Options, out of reach before) and Jak II's multiplayer menus. Elsewhere B still goes back.
- The crosshair is off to start (0.1.3 and 0.1.4 started with the Dot): turn it on in the VR menu's Combat or the
  launcher's VR Controls. A crosshair you chose other than the Dot is kept.
- The game's log: Jak 1's is far shorter (debug lines from the port's base are gone); each game's lists your VR
  options at the start, says where the game and the display were when either stops for over a second, and, on the
  PC, ends with what crashed and where if the game crashes. Its lines are written as they come, so a crash keeps
  the last ones.

Fixed
- Dark Jak's button (Jak II and 3): the left grip makes Dark Jak again, as before 0.1.3: a quick squeeze and let go,
  with no punch or other button. Squeezed again at once and held (Jak 3): Light Jak's powers with a face button or the
  right stick. The left stick's click, where it is Dark Jak, holds it long enough now: a quick click ended before Jak 3
  counted it.
- A colored or black square in front of your face (Jak II and 3): Dark Jak's change and lightning and the game's
  other darkenings of the screen, Jak II's big flashes (explosions) and Jak 3's zoom blur (the red guns' shots, the
  dark gun) were drawn as solid rectangles into the HUD's layer. The darkening dims only the HUD there
  now, the big flashes are drawn in your view, and the zoom blur and slow time's trail are left out of VR (the flat
  screen keeps them).
- The sky against the farthest hills (Jak II and 3, first person): seen at some angles they flickered into each
  other. The sky's depth now follows the eyes' nearer view.
- PC (SteamVR): a small picture of the game, or its HUD, at the end of the gun's laser, where the crosshair is (and
  in place of Show performance's text). Their pictures are written in full now.
- The game's frame rate flipped between the headset's rate and half of it, many times a second on SteamVR (which
  reports a slow frame as a half-rate one): it follows the headset only once a new rate holds for a second.
- A full sound queue froze the game for good when the sound processor stopped answering (a tester's freeze in Jak
  1's Forbidden Jungle temple): a few sounds are skipped instead, and the log says so.
- Jak 3 closed after a few restarts of a mission from the pause menu (a tester's leaper race): during the restart
  the game uploaded its textures with an empty entry, and reading it closed the game. Such entries are passed over
  now, and the log says so.
- Jak II: after failing a mission, walking back to it no longer starts nothing: its scene plays again and the mission
  starts. The multiplayer mod passed over a scene it had just played, a check meant for its guests only.
- Jak II played alone with its multiplayer on (as it starts): what the mod let only a host decide, nobody decided.
  The burning bushes offered no missions; the Baron's squid-shaped boss aimed at the middle of its arena, not at
  you, and its ending never played; the tomb's widow boss threw no bombs (they are what hurt her) and her ending
  never played; the guards never took the Kid or Kor; some enemies (the hoppers) held back. All the game's own again
  out of a multiplayer game. Jak 1's multiplayer likewise changes nothing until you host or join.

0.1.4: more vibration. Still a test release: please keep sending your logs.

0.1.4 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game:
from 0.1.3 it builds again only what changed, from an older version a few minutes). Your saves and options are kept.

New
- Extra vibration (the VR menu's Controls, the launcher's VR Controls), three choices:
  - Off: the vibration as in 0.1.3.
  - Pickups (to start): also a buzz for each thing you collect: a light tick for an orb, an eco pill, ammo or a
    skull gem, a longer one for eco, a strong one for a power cell, a scout fly, a gun or a crystal.
  - The whole world: pickups, and each step (in the foot's hand), each jump, landing, roll and slide, each punch,
    kick, dive and flip, each hit that lands, and the world's shakes (explosions, quakes, bosses).
  Vibration strength sets how hard these are too, and Vibration off turns them off with the rest.
- Logs (Settings, Log): Save log writes everything for a bug report in one file: the app and the headset, each game's
  options, its last crash, the start and the end of its newest log and the run before, the preparation's messages and
  the app's own log. No saves or game files. Report a problem still makes its zip.

Changed
- The Quest's Graphics start as a player's tested Quest 3 has them: Refresh rate 120 Hz (or the most your headset
  has), Smoother frames on, Foveated rendering High, CPU and GPU boost off, Anti-aliasing and Anisotropic filtering off,
  Sharpening Normal. For a game with no options saved yet: options you set are kept. Balanced sets these. The PC's
  are as before.

Fixed
- Quest: the game's log no longer fills with the graphics chip's notes (thousands of lines by the sea), so the lines
  that matter are kept and less is written in play.

0.1.3: Jak II multiplayer, and fixes for the players' reports on 0.1.2. Still a test release: please keep sending
your logs.

0.1.3 changes the game code: each game is prepared again once (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game,
a few minutes, no disc image needed). Your saves and options are kept. From 0.1.3 on, the PC builds again only what
an update changed, so the updates after this one are quicker.

New
- Jak II multiplayer: the community's Jak and Daxter II Multiplayer mod, built in. The title's Multiplayer hosts a
  game or joins one on your network or by address, with every VR feature for everyone and the others shown with their
  VR head and hands. With players of this port only. The launcher's Mods turns it off (then nothing of it runs), as
  Jak 1's multiplayer.
- A crosshair for the guns (Jak II and 3): a mark where the shot would land, on the wall, the ground or the enemy the
  gun points at, the same size near or far, red on an enemy the gun can reach. It starts as a small dot. The VR menu
  and the launcher's VR options have Crosshair (Off, Dot, Cross, Cross and dot, Circle, Circle and dot, Plus),
  Crosshair size, Crosshair color, Crosshair opacity and Crosshair red on enemies.
- Typing with the sticks in a text field (a name, an address): up or down changes the letter, right keeps it, left
  erases one, A enters it and Y leaves.
- Texture packs have their own tab (Texture packs, beside Mods), with Import: a pack you downloaded, as its .zip or
  the folder you unpacked it to (on PC, drop either onto the launcher too). It goes to the game it is for, on, to turn
  off, order or remove there. Packs made for OpenGOAL work; a pack for the PCSX2 emulator, or a .7z or .rar, is told
  apart with what to do. Find texture packs lists the ones in the mod lists.
- PC: the games can be kept on another drive. The Play tab's Where the games are kept moves them (up to about 10 GB a
  game): copied, checked, then removed from where they were. Saves and options stay where they are.

Fixed
- Jak 1's Load Game loads your save (it asked to write over it, and Yes started a new game there).
- Menus: the pointer and the sticks work in every menu of all three games, the trigger clicks with the pointer, and
  the stick's row stays chosen until you point at another. B goes back in the games' menus. Jak II's title: the stick
  and the pointer no longer land on the hidden Secrets row. Every kind of setting (On / Off, lists, sliders) takes the
  pointer, and a long settings page no longer scrolls on by itself when it opens with your hand resting low.
- Both eyes see the same: shiny walls, doors and lanterns, cloth, trails and lasers are drawn alike in each eye. Jak
  3's clothing no longer sticks to your view or the HUD.
- Posters and signs on walls hold steadier against the wall in VR (Jak II's city). The city's lit signs flicker by
  design, as in the game.
- Each shot, its flash and its light start at the gun in your hand.
- Punch and shoot (Jak II and 3's combo, the gun course's lesson): the shot goes to what the punch hit. On foot the
  left fist is only a fist now; Dark Jak (and Jak 3's Light Jak) is the left stick's click there.
- Dark Jak is as solid as Jak; the gear on his arms and legs fades with them near your eyes.
- The JET-Board: a quick squeeze of the right grip takes it out and puts it away, as does the right stick's click; a
  quick click is no longer lost, and it no longer pauses the game with your left thumb on its rest.

Riding
- First person in vehicles is on to start, with Vehicle view height, Vehicle view forward and Vehicle view tilt to put
  the view where you like it.
- Steady view when riding (on to start): the view stays in his seat without each bounce and shake (Jak 3's leaper
  lizards).
- Jak 1's Zoomer in first person: the view no longer drops into the Zoomer at each bump.
- Jak 3's gun turrets (Spargus' wall): you stand at the gun, at Jak's own height, looking over its barrels (the view
  sank into the turret as you played).

Adaptive cutscenes go back to Jak's eyes when a long scene comes back to him, without going back and forth.

Performance (each in the launcher's Graphics)
- Game frame rate: Automatic (to start): the headset's rate while the game keeps up, else 60.
- Dynamic resolution on to start. Share work between eyes (on): the second eye skips what is the same for both.
- Show performance (off): a small line low in your view with the frame rate and each frame's work.
- Less log writing in play.

0.1.1: fixes for the first players' reports, and a pass over how Jak's body feels around you.

Games prepared with 0.1.0 need preparing again (Quest: Prepare again; PC: Build it again on each game).
Your saves and options are kept.

Fixed
- Jak 1's menus: the stick moves through them again, so Load Game loads (it could start a new game by mistake).
- The menu pointer: a hand held still no longer takes back the row the stick chose, on every menu of all three games.
- A long settings page no longer scrolls on by itself when it opens with your hand resting low.
- Jak 1's list settings (language, frame rate, resolution and the like) take the pointer while you change them.
- Jak II no longer crashes as the sewer's gun turret is destroyed; Jak 3 no longer crashes in the arena or at the end
  of its tutorial.
- A scene that ends early lets Jak go again (Jak 1's pipe game in Lost Precursor City could leave him unable to move).
- The Quest no longer runs out of memory preparing Jak 3.
- Start works with Touch controllers on SteamVR: the left stick's click is the menu button there.
- Jak 3's title screen shows its text, and its opening no longer flickers.
- Jak 1's scenes seen through Jak's eyes face what happens.
- The HUD in Jak 1 and Jak 3, and Jak 3's ammo on the gun.
- Jak II's car: its height button works every time (the holsters count only on foot).
- Walking straight walks straight (it looked diagonal).
- Rolling, and punching for real in Jak II and 3, feel as they should.
- The options set at start reach the headset at once.

Your body
- Life size (World scale 100%, on to start): Jak as tall as you, his hands the size of yours.
- Keep your body upright (on): his body stands under your head through kicks, flips and landings.
- Fade body near your eyes (on): a part of him that comes too close fades smoothly instead of filling the view.
- Smoother arms, wrists, knees and fingers; fingers bend against walls and floors; Jak 1's arm wraps follow your hands.

New options (each in the VR menu and the launcher)
- Adaptive cutscenes (on): a scene goes around you by itself while Jak is not properly in it, and back to his eyes.
- Hold the left menu button one second in a scene to switch between his eyes and around you.
- First person in vehicles (off): the Zoomer, the JET-Board, the cars and the like from Jak's seat.
- Spin kick by spreading your arms (off).
- Buzz on d-pad presses and Buzz on gun switch (on).
- Holster size (130% to start: easier to put the gun away).
- Performance: Game frame rate (half rate), Dynamic resolution, PS2 detail, Sharpening and Highest foveation, all off
  or as before to start.

Graphics
- Smoother frames (AppSW) is off to start: it made the title screen flicker.
- Max draw distance now draws everything, shadows too.

Mods
- The Mods section says plainly what works in VR, with Browse mods: install, update, turn off and remove texture packs.
