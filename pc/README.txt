JAK AND DAXTER VR (PC)
An unofficial fan port of Jak and Daxter: The Precursor Legacy, Jak II and Jak 3 to PC VR, on OpenGOAL.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a graphics card with OpenGL 4.3.
- A PC VR headset and its PC software: SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link (Quest by cable or Air Link), Virtual Desktop,
  or any other that offers OpenXR.
- Your own disc image (.iso) of each game you want to play. No game files come with this release.

PLAYING
1. Unzip this folder anywhere you can write to (not Program Files), for example your Documents.
2. Open JakDaxterVR.exe.
3. On the Play tab, press "Set up from your disc image..." under a game and pick its .iso. The game is made from
   your disc once; it takes a few minutes.
4. Start your headset's PC VR software, then press PLAY IN VR.

Your set-up, saves and choices are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\JakQuestVR, so an update (the Play tab's Updates card, or
a new zip unzipped over this folder) keeps them. The VR options are on the Graphics, VR Body and VR Controls tabs and
in the game's own VR menu. The Controls tab shows every controller's buttons.

MODS
The Mods tab has Jak 1's Multiplayer (on to start; it changes nothing until you host or join a game in the VR menu)
and texture packs for each game (OpenGOAL's .zip packs; adding one means building the game again, which the tab
offers). MODS.md says more; party-server\MULTIPLAYER.md is how to play together and run your own party server.

A PROBLEM?
The Logs tab's "Gather the logs" makes one zip in Downloads\JakQuestVR: post it with what you were doing in the
project's GitHub issues.
