## First thing next: the shape under the eyes looking down is NOT fixed

gk build 33 (the Merc2 slot fallback below) built, and tests/jak1_down.sim ran into C:\JakQuest\sim\j1_down7 with
JAKVR_BODY_TRACE=1. Result: j1_down7\b-down-left.png still shows, right under the eyes, Jak's collar seen from above
(brown shoulder pads, blue tunic, the skin of his shoulders, and the open neck hole with two dark holes in it). The
"sends no matrix" line never appears in run.log, so slot 7 (the neck) is sent every frame and the stale-slot theory is
wrong. The fallback code is harmless and can stay or go.

The lead for next time: run.log's trace puts the posed neck base 16 cm behind the eyes and 4 cm below, and the chest
18 cm behind (flat frame: right, up, ahead), where the eyes looking 60 degrees down could not see it. But the trace also
says the game's own neck was 9 cm below the eyes and 5 cm ahead, which is where the picture shows the collar. So the
eyes seem to draw Jak's body with the spine as the game posed it, not as body() moved it back (the arms may follow while
the hips and chest do not). Check, in order:
1. Whether the trace's frame is the flat heading or the pitched head (if pitched, recompute the places).
2. Whether Jak 1's merc matrices for the eyes are made from the bones before vr-body-update! writes them (bones.gc's
   bone calculation, against update-camera in the frame), or from a copy (the shadow and the eyes may differ).
3. Whether body() writes the moved hips and chest to the bones it reads, or only to its own working copy (BodyState).
A quick picture test: JAKVR_BODY_TRACE=1 with Body forward at -30 (vr-options.txt body-forward) should move the collar
in the picture if the drawn mesh follows body(); if it does not move, the drawn mesh ignores the moved spine.

## Done this session (all three games compile; gk builds)

- **Scenes in VR**: the title, the menus and pause stay stereo 3D around the player, with the pause menu on the HUD
  panel (sim j1_body3/4). Cutscenes follow the cutscene option.
- **Body IK refined against Ratchet** (item 23): hips eased Ratchet's way (35 degree dead zone, 4 or 8 rad/s), the
  chest twist split over upper_body and chest, the legs bent from the moved hips to the game's feet (two-bone, soft IK,
  knee forward), arms with the least turn, the elbow pole, the shoulder lifting and reaching, soft IK then up to 15%
  stretch, half the hand's roll on the forearm; the head turned for the shadow; the shadow keeps the head.
- **Hands drawn where the controllers will be** at display time (the runtime's own prediction a refresh ahead;
  xr.h locate_grips, option predict-hands, on). The aim poses ride on them (Rig map_aim).
- **Neck straightened** on the chest as made before the body is fitted (jak_vr.cpp straighten, body step 3), so an
  idle's neck bend no longer moves the collar. Trace confirmed: neck 18 cm behind the eyes looking down, chest 19 cm.
- **The shape under the eyes looking down** (the "neck stump"): proven to be Jak's own model (JAKVR_HIDE_TEST=eichar
  removed it). A fix was tried for a suspected cause (the hidden head shrinking to a neck slot the model never sends):
  Merc2 remembers which slots were sent and falls back to the chest's matrix (FrameTag::Hidden root_alt; merc_hidden
  gained `root_alt`). The test showed that was not the cause; see "First thing next".
- **Body forward** option (centimetres, as Ratchet's), beside Body height.
- **Hands against the world** (item 25): four sphere probes a frame asked by the runtime (pc-vr-hand-probes!) and swept
  by each game (vr-hand-probes!: Jak 1 fill-and-probe-using-line-sphere, Jak II and 3 a collide-query). The palm stops at
  what it meets, the fingers curl over what is in front of them. VrBodyInfo grew probe fields (now 1728 bytes, the
  same struct in the three pc-vr.gc files).
- **Gun in the hand** (items 27, 28): the gun's bones carried on to the gun joint as posed now (gun-util.gc
  vr-gun-fill!, body step 7), so it no longer trails the hand by a frame; drawn, with Aim with your hand, the hand turns
  so the barrel (gun bone 13) points along the right controller's aim pose plus Gun angle, and the shot and laser go
  there (vr-gun-aim! at the end of each gun's post). **Two-hand grip** as Ratchet's support grip: the left grip squeezed
  at the gun's front (6 to 40 cm ahead along the aim, within 12 cm) holds it, not L2; the gun then points from the right
  hand to the left, the left hand stays on it; a squeeze begun elsewhere stays L2. Right fingers close on the handle.
- **Haptics** (item 22): Vibration strength (25 to 200%) on every buzz; World vibration (on) for a hand meeting a wall
  (by speed) and pressing on it (Ratchet's feel_walls); the game's big motor to both hands, the small one to the acting
  hand (gun hand, last punch, else both); a kick per round fired by gun (pc-vr-gun-fired! from target-gun-fire in
  Jak II and 3; the left hand a share with both on the gun).
- **Graphics options**: refresh-hz and resolution already reach OpenXR from vr-options.txt; the owner's reminder ("90 hz,
  120 hz, resolution, etc.") is in item 36: the launcher's Graphics tab still has to offer them.
- **Multiplayer research** (item 39): the mod is Teamruns (Jak 1 only). Plan and the open permission question are in
  the list.
